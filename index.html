Deposits & cancellations

  • Deposit: a 50% deposit is required to confirm your appointment. The remaining balance is due when the job is finished. You can pay it by card through Pay Online.
  • 48+ hours' notice: if you cancel or reschedule with at least 48 hours' notice, your deposit carries over to your new date, or you can request a refund.
  • Less than 48 hours' notice: the deposit is non-refundable.
  • No-show or no access: if we arrive and can't access the vehicle, the deposit is non-refundable.
  • If we cancel: you get a full refund or a free reschedule.
